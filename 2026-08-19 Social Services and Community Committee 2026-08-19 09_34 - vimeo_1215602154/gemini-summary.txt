Submitters Angela Maynard (Tenants Protection Association), Presbyterian Support Otago, NZCCSS, Tenants Union, and Serena Gibbon (Tenancy Advisory Limited) supported the boarding house registration bill. Maynard said current fines are too low, proposing $50,000 to $100,000. NZCCSS said some rents reach $450 weekly. Submitters said the register requires transition runways.
